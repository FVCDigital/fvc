## How to say it (keep it casual)

Preferred plain lines for calls, emails and announcements. Do not over-explain the tech.

- **Own money (the killer line).** "We are not raising a fund that lends other people's money. It is our own money." Say it early when someone says CIS, collective investment, or fund licence.
- **Loan Registry.** "…with the Loan Registry recording each move." That is enough. Do not open with contracts, events, or Hardhat numbers unless someone asks.
- **Full repayment path in one breath.** SMEs receive funding and repay in pounds through our payments partner, with the Loan Registry recording each move.
- **Token.** Utility token: staking rewards in USDC from lending income, selected governance, early access. Not equity. Not a claim on SME loans.
- **Not a fund.** Company capital on the company balance sheet. Buying or staking FVC does not put money into a pooled loan vehicle. Token holders are not LPs in the loan book.
- **VC vs lender (why we push back).** "First Venture Capital" is the brand. The book is company balance-sheet funding, not a VC fund of outside LPs. That is why fund authorisation does not map onto this model.
