### openPR (openpr.com)

Strict on promotional language, so keep it factual and drop adjectives like "open" and "transparent" from the headline.

- Headline: FVC Develops Public Loan Registry and Repayment Tracking for Interest-Free SME Funding
- Summary: First Venture Capital is building a loan registry, automated repayment reconciliation and security testing for funding small businesses without interest.
- Opening: FVC Asset Group Ltd, the company behind First Venture Capital (FVC), is developing a system for funding startups and small and medium-sized businesses through revenue-sharing agreements and treasury-backed grants rather than interest. Each funding agreement and repayment is designed to be recorded on a public blockchain.
- Body: full release.
