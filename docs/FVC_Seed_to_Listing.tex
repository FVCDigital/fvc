\section{Summary}

FVC funds small and medium-sized businesses without charging interest. Each business pays back a share of its revenue, and part of those repayments is paid to FVC holders who stake. The token, the sale and investor vesting have been live on Ethereum since March 2026.

Seed buyers enter below every later round. The default price is \textbf{\$0.03} per FVC, and the lead tranche is \textbf{\$0.025}, half the \textbf{\$0.05} listing target. Price, lock period and release schedule are negotiable for larger tickets, and the live contract already supports bespoke terms for each investor.

\section{The token}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item \textbf{Standard:} ERC-20 on Ethereum, capped at 1,000,000,000 FVC.
\item \textbf{Payment:} USDC, USDT or ETH. The cash moves to a multisig treasury in the same transaction.
\item \textbf{Price:} \$0.03 by default. Larger tickets can negotiate a lower price, down to the \$0.025 lead tranche.
\item \textbf{Terms:} price, lock period and release schedule are agreed per investor and set on-chain for that wallet.
\item \textbf{Delivery:} tokens go straight into the vesting contract, not into a wallet that can sell them.
\item \textbf{Seed allocation:} 100 million FVC, 10\% of supply.
\end{itemize}

\section{Roadmap}

\begin{enumerate}
\tightlist
\item \textbf{Seed, open now.} From \$0.025. This is the round a fund would join.
\item \textbf{Public presale, after the seed.} 150 million FVC across six tiers, from \$0.030 to \$0.048, averaging about \$0.0405. Lock: 6 months, then 6 months of release.
\item \textbf{Listing, after the presale.} Target price \$0.05, a \$50 million fully diluted valuation. Exchange applications prepared for KuCoin and Bitget target a window of Q4 2026 to Q1 2027, subject to the presale and exchange approval.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Where the money goes}

The largest single use of the \$8.93 million pre-listing raise is the business funding pool: \textbf{40\%, about \$3.6 million}. Each business signs an interest-free agreement and pays a share of its revenue back to the treasury. The treasury pays part of those repayments to stakers in USDC, and uses the rest for reserves and buying back FVC. Staking is built and tested, and it goes live with the first repayments.

The rest covers engineering and the audit, exchange listings and liquidity, marketing, and legal work. Hashlock is auditing the contracts now.

\section{Vesting while the raise and listing are ahead}

The lock runs per purchase. The clock starts on the day that buyer pays, not on one date for the whole round. The default terms are already in the live contract:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item \textbf{First 12 months:} nothing can be claimed. A purchase in October 2026 stays locked until October 2027.
\item \textbf{Next 12 months:} tokens release in a straight line and are fully released 24 months after purchase.
\item \textbf{Claiming:} the buyer claims each release. The schedule is visible at \href{https://fvcdigital.com/buy-fvc}{fvcdigital.com/buy-fvc}.
\item \textbf{Listing:} a listing in the first year does not unlock anything. That keeps seed tokens out of the first trading.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{These terms are negotiable.} A larger ticket can agree a shorter lock, a different release length and a lower price. The contract sets those terms for that investor's wallet alone, so a bespoke deal needs no new contract.
